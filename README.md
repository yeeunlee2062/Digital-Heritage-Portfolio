# Digital Heritage Portfolio

[![checks](https://github.com/yeeunlee2062/Digital-Heritage-Portfolio/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/yeeunlee2062/Digital-Heritage-Portfolio/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

I develop research software with humanities and heritage researchers, creating data models, processing pipelines, and interfaces that turn their research questions into practical tools. I rigorously evaluate these tools and publish them to enable inspection and reuse by others. In projects spanning tangible, intangible, and natural heritage, I ensure that each computational result—whether a 3D reconstruction, a recommendation, or a generated image—remains transparently connected to its underlying sources, methodological decisions, and associated uncertainties.

## Research Software Practice

- **Code to look at first**: the [volumetric reconstruction pipeline](02_chunaengjeon-4d/pipeline/) (camera registration, per-frame 3DGS training, a quantised web format with a reference decoder), the [interpretation platform](02_chunaengjeon-4d/platform/) and its WebGL2 player, the [linked-data model with SHACL validation](01_hansan-mosi-scene-archive/data-model/), and the [reproducibility package](03_nexus-natural-heritage/reproducibility/) of the NEXUS article.
- **Tests and CI**: the [test suite](tests/) runs on every push. It checks the splat format against its quantization bounds and the published frames, the integrity of the dance database and its frame ranges, SHACL conformance and JSON-LD/Turtle equivalence of the archive records, byte-identical reproduction of the NEXUS results with checksums, and every relative link in the documentation. Run it with `pip install "./tests[test]"` and `pytest tests`.
- **Working with researchers**: requirements come from expert interviews, Kano–QFD analysis, competency questions agreed before implementation, and the researchers' own workbooks as the source of truth.
- **Reproducible and FAIR**: deterministic builds that record tool versions and input hashes, SHA-256 fixity, linked-data publication with DCAT and content negotiation, an open license and citation metadata. Requirements not yet met, and partial results, are stated on the project pages.

## Repository Scope and Attribution

This repository presents selected research implementations and their supporting materials rather than complete project archives. Publicly available resources include case-study descriptions, visualizations, metadata models, annotation and validation materials, reconstruction code, and documented reproducibility packages. Raw volumetric captures, personal information, licensed data, and some unpublished research resources remain in their respective project environments. Individual project pages specify the evidence that can be inspected and the limitations of its release.

Project-specific research roles and partner institutions are described in [CONTRIBUTIONS.md](CONTRIBUTIONS.md) using the CRediT taxonomy, together with a statement on the use of AI coding assistants for code design. Citation metadata for this repository is provided in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Repository code is licensed under [MIT](LICENSE), while author-owned text and figures are made available under [CC BY 4.0](CONTRIBUTIONS.md#licences); third-party materials remain subject to their original conditions of use.

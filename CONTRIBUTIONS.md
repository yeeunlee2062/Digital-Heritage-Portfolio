# Contributions

Roles follow the [CRediT taxonomy](https://credit.niso.org/), given in parentheses after each task. "YL" is Yeeun Lee. All projects were carried out at the Heritage Information Lab, Korea National University of Heritage, under the supervision of the lab's Principal Investigator, who also secured the funding. In each project, YL designed the research and carried it through from data modeling to implementation and evaluation, working with the partner institutions listed below.
